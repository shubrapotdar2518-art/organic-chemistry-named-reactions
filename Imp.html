Aldol Condensation

1. Used to form β-hydroxy aldehydes/ketones

2. Important in making complex organic molecules

3. Used in synthesis of resins, perfumes, and pharmaceuticals

Friedel-Crafts

1. Used to introduce alkyl or acyl group into benzene

2. Important in making aromatic compounds

3. Used in synthesis of dyes, drugs, and plastics

Cannizzaro

1. Converts aldehydes into alcohol and acid (no α-H)

2. Used in preparation of benzyl alcohol and benzoic acid

3. Important in industrial organic synthesis

Wurtz Reaction

1. Used to prepare higher alkanes from alkyl halides

2. Helps in carbon–carbon bond formation

3. Used in laboratory synthesis of symmetrical alkanes

Sandmeyer

1. Used to replace diazonium group with Cl, Br, CN

2. Helps in synthesis of haloarenes and nitriles

3. Important in dye and pharmaceutical industries

Rosenmund Reduction

1. Converts acid chlorides to aldehydes

2. Used in synthesis of aromatic aldehydes

3. Important in making perfumes and flavoring compounds

Hofmann Rearrangement

1. Converts amides to primary amines (with one less carbon)

2. Used in preparation of amines in organic synthesis

3. Important in making drugs and agrochemicals

Reimer-Tiemann

1. Used to prepare salicylaldehyde from phenol

2. Important in making flavoring agents and perfumes

3. Used in synthesis of dyes and medicines

Finkelstein Reaction

1. Used to prepare alkyl iodides from alkyl chlorides/bromides

2. Helpful in nucleophilic substitution reactions

3. Used in synthesis of pharmaceutical intermediates
