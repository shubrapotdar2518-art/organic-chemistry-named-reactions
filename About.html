Project Description

The Virtual Chemistry Lab is a digital platform that simulates real-world chemistry experiments in an interactive environment. It allows students to explore chemical reactions, understand concepts visually, and perform experiments without physical lab setups. This enhances conceptual clarity and makes learning engaging and accessible.

Purpose of the Project

The need for a Virtual Chemistry Lab arises due to limited access to physical laboratories, safety concerns, and resource constraints. This project provides a safe, cost-effective, and flexible learning environment where students can practice experiments anytime, anywhere. It also helps in reinforcing theoretical knowledge through virtual hands-on experience.
