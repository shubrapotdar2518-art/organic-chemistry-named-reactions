Aldol Condensation

A reaction where aldehydes or ketones with α-hydrogen combine to form β-hydroxy compounds which further dehydrate into α,β-unsaturated compounds.
EnolateCondensationβ-hydroxy

Friedel–Crafts Reaction

An electrophilic aromatic substitution where alkyl or acyl groups are introduced into a benzene ring using catalysts like AlCl₃.
AromaticAlCl₃Substitution

Cannizzaro Reaction

A redox reaction where aldehydes without α-hydrogen form alcohol and acid.
No α-HRedoxDisproportionation

Wurtz Reaction

Two alkyl halides react with sodium metal to form higher alkanes.
Na metalCouplingAlkane

Sandmeyer Reaction

Diazonium salts are replaced by halides using copper salts.
DiazoniumCu saltsSubstitution

Rosenmund Reduction

Reduction of acid chlorides into aldehydes using hydrogen and Pd catalyst.
Pd catalystAldehyde

Hofmann Rearrangement

Amide converts into amine with one less carbon atom.
AmideAmine

Reimer–Tiemann Reaction

Phenol reacts to form salicylaldehyde by adding CHO group.
PhenolCHO

Finkelstein Reaction

Halide exchange reaction forming alkyl iodides.
