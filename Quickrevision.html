Named Reactions at a Glance

1. Aldol Condensation

Reagents: Aldehyde/Ketone + Base

Product: β-hydroxy compound

2. Friedel-Crafts Acylation

Reagents: Benzene + Acyl chloride + AlCl₃

Product: Aromatic ketone

3. Cannizzaro Reaction

Reagents: Aldehyde (no α-H) + Base

Product: Alcohol + Acid

4. Wurtz Reaction

Reagents: Alkyl halide + Na

Product: Higher alkane

5. Sandmeyer Reaction

Reagents: Diazonium salt + CuCl

Product: Aryl halide

6. Rosenmund Reduction

Reagents: Acid chloride + H₂/Pd

Product: Aldehyde

7. Hofmann Rearrangement

Reagents: Amide + Br₂ + NaOH

Product: Amine

8. Reimer-Tiemann Reaction

Reagents: Phenol + CHCl₃

Product: Salicylaldehyde

9. Finkelstein Reaction

Reagents: Alkyl halide + NaI

Product: Alkyl iodide
